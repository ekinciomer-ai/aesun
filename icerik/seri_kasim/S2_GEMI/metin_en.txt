How do you get seventy warships past a giant chain? You drag them over a hill!
April 1453. The siege of Constantinople.
The Byzantines had sealed their harbor, the Golden Horn, with a massive iron chain.
The Ottoman fleet tried to break it... and failed.
So the 21-year-old Sultan Mehmed came up with a crazy plan: move the ships over land.
Behind Galata, his men laid a track of wooden logs... and greased them.
Ships were hauled uphill by men and oxen. Some say with their sails up and drums beating!
On the morning of April 22nd, the defenders woke up... and the enemy fleet was inside their harbor.
The chain was suddenly useless. One more stretch of wall had to be defended.
Thirty-seven days later, the city fell. Would you have thought of this? Tell me below.
