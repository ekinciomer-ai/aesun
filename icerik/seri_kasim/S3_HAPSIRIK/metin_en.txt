You sneeze, and someone says "Bless you." But why?
Because a sneeze once looked like a death sentence.
Rome, the year 590. A terrible plague hits the city.
People sneezed... and soon after, they died. Even the Pope died of it.
The story goes that the new Pope, Gregory, told people to answer every sneeze with a prayer.
But the habit is even older. Romans told a sneezer "Salve!" Stay healthy!
In ancient Greece, a sneeze was a sign from the gods. Good or bad... depending on when it came.
And "Gesundheit"? It's German for "health". Immigrants brought it to America.
Same idea everywhere: one sneeze, one wish for your life.
