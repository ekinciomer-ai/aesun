Everyone thinks this is a farmer and his wife. They're not!
American Gothic. 1930. Painted by Grant Wood.
The woman? His own sister, Nan. She was thirty.
The man next to her? Wood's dentist! Sixty-two years old.
They never stood side by side. Each one posed alone.
Newspapers called it "An Iowa Farmer and His Wife"... and Nan was furious.
She said it was a father and his daughter. Wood never gave a straight answer.
The house is real. A tiny farmhouse in Eldon, Iowa... with a church-style Gothic window.
Iowans hated it. One farm wife threatened to bite Wood's ear off!
