Why is a marathon exactly 26.2 miles? Blame the British royal family!
Early Olympic marathons were about 40 kilometers. No fixed distance.
London, 1908. The start line was placed on the lawn of Windsor Castle.
The finish? Inside the stadium, right in front of the Royal Box.
Between the two: 26 miles and 385 yards.
And that race became a legend. Italy's Dorando Pietri entered the stadium first... and turned the wrong way.
He collapsed five times. Officials kept lifting him up.
That help got him disqualified, and the gold went to an American, Johnny Hayes.
But the next day, Queen Alexandra gave Pietri a special cup. In 1921, the distance became official.
So every marathon from New York to London is run to a royal box. Have you ever run one?
